## Governance and safety copy

### Global prototype notice

**Portfolio interface**  
This experience presents Collective AI's product and division architecture. Product status, availability, and deployment readiness vary by division.

### Regulated-system notice

**Review required**  
Medical, legal, financial, energy, autonomous, robotic, and security systems remain subject to licensed professional review, jurisdiction-specific requirements, privacy controls, and safety clearance.

### Simulation notice

**Simulation mode**  
This product is represented as a simulator, admin shell, research environment, or reporting workflow. It is not cleared for live execution.

### Legal notice

Juris Guard provides legal intelligence and assistive review. It does not replace licensed legal counsel.

### Health notice

Vital Helix and Eon Core surface health and longevity intelligence. They do not diagnose or treat, and intervention guidance requires appropriate clinical review.

### Financial notice

Quantum Ledger interfaces are informational unless and until custody, suitability, execution, and jurisdictional controls are cleared.

### Mobility notice

Nomad Nexus provides mobility intelligence, not immigration or tax advice. Visa and entry requirements must be verified with official government, embassy, or consular sources.

### Behavioral notice

Cognara Mind measures behavior; it does not diagnose or manipulate. Behavioral data remains governed by informed sovereignty, data minimalism, and exit architecture.
